How Dyson Air Halo™ works

Instead of a single, concentrated jet of hot air, Air Halo™ projects hundreds of micro-jets from a circular halo ring. These streams converge into a controlled, low-turbulence airflow that surrounds the head – speeding up drying while helping prevent hot spots.

  • 360° halo airflow – wraps the head in diffused air for even drying.
  • Intelligent heat sensors – measure air temperature many times a second.
  • Digital motor control – delivers fast airflow with a compact, balanced feel.
